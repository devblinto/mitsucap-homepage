TRAC Lease

A flexible lease structure designed for qualified motor vehicles and trailers, with an agreed end-of-term residual value used to determine the equipment’s final market value adjustment.

Schedule a Free Consultation

Financing Built For Commercial Vehicles

A TRAC lease is generally used for "over-the-road" or qualified motor vehicles and trailers.

Lessor and Lessee agree on the equipment’s estimated residual or predicted fair market value.

Residual Value Comparison

At the end of the lease term the estimated residual value is compared to the actual fair market value, as determined by the sale to the lessee, to a third party, or by a registered appraiser.

Discuss Your Equipment Needs

When Actual And Estimated Values Differ

Lower Actual Residual Value

If, at the end of the lease term, the actual residual value is less than the estimated residual value, the lessee will make up the difference.

Higher Actual Residual Value

The opposite would apply if the actual residual value is greater than the estimated residual value, the lessee will receive the difference.

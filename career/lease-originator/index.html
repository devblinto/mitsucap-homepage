  • Department: Sales
  • Location: US
  • Posted October 2, 2017
Apply Now View All Positions

Responsibilities

As a Lease Originator, you will build relationships with businesses and vendors and help them finance the equipment they need to grow.

  • Identify and develop new equipment leasing and financing opportunities with businesses and vendors.
  • Build and maintain long-term relationships with clients, vendors and referral partners.
  • Understand each client’s equipment needs and recommend the right lease or finance structure.
  • Prepare proposals and credit applications, and guide clients from application to funding.
  • Work closely with the credit and operations teams to deliver a smooth client experience.
  • Meet monthly and quarterly origination goals.

Skills/Experience

We are looking for a motivated sales professional who enjoys solving equipment and financing challenges for business owners.

  • 2+ years of experience in equipment leasing, commercial lending or B2B sales.
  • Understanding of lease structures such as FMV, TRAC and capital leases.
  • Strong communication, negotiation and presentation skills.
  • Self-motivated and able to manage a sales pipeline independently.
  • Proficiency with CRM software and Microsoft Office.
  • Bachelor’s degree in business, finance or a related field preferred.
